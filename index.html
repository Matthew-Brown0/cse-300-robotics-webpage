Why care about Robotics?

As we enter an era or science and engineering and technology progresses far beyond the limits of what we once thought possible, there is an every growing need to push the boundaries of what we know. We have a need to explore, expand, and research the cutting endge of technology.

Robotics is a field that offers great potential for growth. We see its influences every day in countless industries. Because of this there is a near insatiable need for new talent and skills amoung youth and young adults.

With the advent of Artifitial Intelligence and its recent surge in capabilities over the last few years, the outer edges of the limits in robotics have been pushed farther once again. Just to name one set of examples, companies such as Waymo and Tesla have made heavy use of AI to create self driving cars that can uniquely respond and control its robotic vehicles to drive individuals safely to their location.
